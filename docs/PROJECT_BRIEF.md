# GardenTwin project brief

## Idea

GardenTwin is an AI-powered climate-resilient garden simulator for home gardeners and community gardens. It will explore how weather, soil, irrigation and fertilization affect a growing season, and help users compare strategies for conserving water and growing food more sustainably.

## Initial pilot

- Track: AI + Climate.
- Crop: tomato.
- Location: Edison, New Jersey.
- User: a home gardener or community garden organizer.
- Data direction: historical weather and publicly available local soil information.
- Runtime direction: Ayaan's locally run backend and AI model.

## Intended experience

Users choose location, plant, planting date, soil conditions, watering schedule and fertilizer usage. A day-by-day simulation and draggable timeline show plant development and environmental stress. Graphs communicate soil moisture, temperature, nutrients and predicted plant health. Users compare management strategies under the same historical conditions.

The intended AI component analyzes conditions and recommends irrigation and fertilization strategies. The team will decide which parts can be implemented and evaluated during the hackathon.

## Climate goals

- Help people explore adaptation to heat, drought and variable weather.
- Reduce unnecessary irrigation.
- Explore more efficient fertilizer use and potential nutrient-runoff reduction.

These are project goals; measured benefits must come from the team's evaluation.

## Evaluation direction

Compare AI-assisted irrigation with fixed watering schedules and simple baseline predictions. Use later, unseen historical dates for predictive tests. Report estimated water savings with the simulation model and assumptions documented. Separate predictive accuracy, simulated outcomes and field measurements in the write-up.
